# AnswerRadar

**[AnswerRadar](https://answerradar.net)** is an independent, data-first directory of
AI-visibility and **GEO** (generative engine optimization) tools — software that tracks and
improves how your brand shows up in AI answers from **ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI
Overviews, Gemini and Copilot**.

- 🔗 **Website:** https://answerradar.net
- 🧭 **Free AI Visibility Checker:** https://answerradar.net/ai-visibility-checker
- 📚 **Guides:** https://answerradar.net/guides

## What's inside
- Side-by-side **comparisons** of AI-visibility & GEO tools
- **Best-of lists** by use case (enterprise, small business, free, ChatGPT tracking, and more)
- Independent, **dated reviews** — every entry verified, never copied from vendor sites
- A free, no-signup **AI Visibility Checker**

## Why it exists
More buyers now ask AI assistants for recommendations instead of scrolling search results.
AnswerRadar helps brands understand — and improve — whether those assistants mention and
recommend them.
